Surveillance severity

These labels come from Overdrive and filter Surveillance and Proximity recordings. With none selected, severity filtering is disabled; recordings without a reported severity are kept.

Notice covers background or passing activity; Alert marks nearby or approaching activity; Critical marks the closest or strongest reported threat. This archive only filters the label reported by Overdrive.

06

Local retention

Remove older local archive copies by category and optionally limit total storage usage.

Vehicle recordings stay untouched Retention deletes only local archive copies. It never deletes or changes recordings stored in the vehicle. Saving settings applies enabled retention rules immediately to local files.
Retention by category

Items protected by “Keep the latest” are never deleted by age or by the storage limit.

Age uses the original recording time when available, then falls back to the local archive time.

Storage limit

Archive filesystem capacity is not available yet.
